About

I am a Ph.D. student in Operatons Research at the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology, where I am advised by Professor Guanghui Lan.

My research interests lie broadly in mathematical optimization and machine learning, with an emphasis on large-scale optimization algorithms, variational inequalities, and stochastic optimization. I am particularly interested in developing theoretically grounded methods that are computationally efficient and applicable to large-scale problems.
